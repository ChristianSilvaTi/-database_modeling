A medical clinic wants to develop a database system to manage its patients, doctors, and appointments.

The clinic employs several doctors, each specializing in a specific field. A doctor can attend to multiple patients, and a patient can schedule appointments with different doctors.

Each appointment must record:
Date
Time
Reason for the visit
Appointment status (Scheduled, Completed, Canceled)

A patient can have multiple phone numbers, and each phone number must be stored separately.

The clinic also wants to maintain a registry of its medical specialties. A specialty can be associated with multiple doctors, but each doctor has only one primary specialty.

Additionally, after a completed appointment, the doctor may issue a prescription for the patient. An appointment can have zero or multiple prescriptions, and each prescription must store:
Medication name
Dosage
Duration of the treatment
